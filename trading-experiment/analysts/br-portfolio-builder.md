## 6. Tax efficiency strategy (taxable account)

1. **Turnover restraint *is* the tax strategy at this size.** Nine trades in six weeks; every lot is short-term, taxed at ordinary income. The single largest tax lever available is not harvesting or lot selection — it is continuing to say no. Every avoided trade is a deferred short-term gain.
2. **Long-term-capital-gains clock — nothing is close.** Earliest eligibility: NVDA 2027-07-10, VTI 2027-07-16, OMCL 2027-07-18, XLE 2027-07-29, VXUS 2027-07-31. **Implication:** any trim before mid-2027 is short-term by construction, so LTCG timing should not influence sizing decisions this year. It *should* influence one thing — **prefer trimming the highest-cost-basis lot** (VTI's 8/7-remaining lots, HIFO) when a trim is needed for funding, which the GEHC trigger's paired VTI trim already does by default.
3. **The only harvestable loss is OMCL (−23.7%, ~−$1.19 unrealized) and it should not be harvested.** The tax benefit is a few cents; the cost is closing the book's widest-margin-of-safety position (MS: ~48.7% upside) and triggering a 30-day wash-sale window that would block a re-entry on exactly the catalyst GS says is the highest-EV place for the next satellite dollar. **Harvesting here would be the tax tail wagging the investment dog.**
4. **VXUS foreign tax credit:** foreign withholding at this position size is measured in cents and falls far under the $300 single-filer de minimis, so the credit is claimable directly on Schedule 3 without Form 1116. No action, no complexity — just don't leave it unclaimed.
5. **Qualified dividends:** VTI/VXUS distributions are largely qualified, but the >60-day holding-period requirement per ex-date must be met — another reason not to trim core around distribution dates. **VTI/VXUS both go ex-dividend in late September;** avoid discretionary core trims in that window absent a fired trigger.

## 7. Dollar-cost-averaging plan for redeploying profits

The book's deployable capital is $50 base **plus accumulated profits only.** Current accumulated profit: **~$0.50** (pool $50.4981 − $50.00 base).

**Standing DCA rule (unchanged, still not yet triggerable):**
- **Tranche size $2.50.** Profits accumulate un-deployed until cumulative profit reaches **≥$2.50**, then one tranche is released. No partial tranches — sub-$2.50 deployments are commission-free but not free: they add positions and monitoring load without moving any weight meaningfully.
- **Destination: the most-underweight *vetted* sleeve line at release time** — today that would be OMCL (−2.45pp, and the only holding trading below its own DCF fair value). NVDA is excluded as a DCA destination while MS's overvaluation call stands, regardless of its headline gap. **A DCA rule that mechanically buys the most-underweight name is a rule that buys whatever your valuation desk hates most; the vetted-only filter is what stops that.**
- **Cadence: at most one tranche per calendar month**, and never during an un-resolved binary-event window on the destination name.
- **Status: ~$0.50 accumulated — no tranche available. ~$2.00 of further profit required.** Realistically 4–8 weeks away at the book's current trajectory. No action.

## 9. Investment Policy Statement — one page, as of 2026-08-20

**Objective.** Compound a $50 trading pool (inside a ~$100 taxable cash account, ~$50 untouchable reserve) at an aggressive risk tolerance over a short-to-medium horizon, building a durable long-run track record. Equities and ETFs only; no options; fractional shares permitted.

**Strategic allocation (% of pool).** Core 55% — VTI 30%, VXUS 25%. Satellite 35% — NVDA 15% *(under formal review, decision 2026-08-27, default revision to 11%)*, OMCL 10%, XLE 10%. Cash 10%, treated as a hard floor (~$5.05), not a residual.

**Permitted actions.** (1) Trigger fires — execute the pre-committed action. (2) DCA tranche release at ≥$2.50 accumulated profit, vetted destinations only, max one per month. (3) A new position that has cleared full rule-6 cross-vetting (MS DCF + BW risk read) and passes rule-2 entry discipline, funded without breaching the cash floor. **Nothing else.**

**Prohibited.** Adding to a position your own valuation desk calls overvalued. Chasing a move already 3%+ priced in. Opening a single-name position during an active geopolitical shock. Breaching the cash floor for a discretionary entry. Buying because a clock ran out (rule 16). Sizing off any price not live-verified via Robinhood (rule 4).

**Review cadence.** Daily monitor via this desk; monthly full re-underwrite (next 2026-09-01); triggers evaluated every scheduled run regardless of news flow (rule 12).

**Expected outcomes.** Return ~11–15% annualized at current weights; bad-year pool drawdown −20% to −28%, severe −32% to −40%, halved at the account level by the reserve.

**Governing principle.** *Every deadline expires to no-action. Every repeated flag converts to a rule or retires. The target you cannot reach under your own rules is not a target — it is an error in the policy, and the policy is what gets corrected.*
