- **Trade policy surge, still building.** Global trade-policy activity (tariff hikes, import bans, subsidies) through mid-2026 is running roughly double 2024 levels and ~25% above the 2025 average — the highest since tracking began post-2008 — with restrictive measures dominating and preliminary nowcasting pointing to *more* activity into H2 2026, not less. New US tariffs now cover >99% of imports across the top 60 trading partners, mostly in the 10-12.5% range. For this book: VXUS's ex-US diversification benefit is real but not a free pass from tariff friction — global growth itself is being revised down (global growth now projected ~2.5% for 2026, below the earlier ~3.0% estimate, explicitly attributed in part to the Middle East conflict's energy-price effects) alongside the trade-policy drag.
- **Rates: the long end is the story now, not just the Fed.** The 30-year Treasury's move to a 19-year high (~5.26-5.29%, last seen 2007) is being read as investors pricing in higher inflation, larger government borrowing, and prolonged restrictive policy — a term-premium/fiscal story more than a near-term-Fed-path story. This matters directly for NVDA's DCF (higher discount rate compresses the fair-value estimate further, independent of any operating-fundamentals change) and for VTI/VXUS's growth-heavy composition generally. It also plainly favors income-generating and cash-like exposure at the margin, which this book already holds via its 10% cash-floor policy.
- **Energy/geopolitical premium is no longer a tail scenario, it's live.** The Hormuz disruption (traffic down ~90% from pre-war levels per GS) and today's ceasefire-framework lapse mean the oil-price and tanker-freight-rate premium GS is now trading (FRO/DHT) is a current fact, not a hedge against a hypothetical. XLE captures a diluted version of this exposure already; this desk continues to view that as sufficient satellite exposure to the theme without adding a second, more concentrated vehicle (see §1).
- **Net read for this book's policy:** none of the above changes a single target percentage this run. The core/satellite split and the 15/10/10 satellite framework were built for exactly this kind of environment (aggressive mandate, expect volatility, hold the line on process). The one live watch item is the 30-year yield's trajectory feeding into rule 6a, flagged in §5.
