(3) A new position that has cleared full rule-6 cross-vetting (MS DCF + BW risk read) and passes rule-2 entry discipline, funded without breaching the cash floor. **Nothing else.**

**Prohibited.** Adding to a position your own valuation desk calls overvalued. Chasing a move already 3%+ priced in — including a no-catalyst bounce like today's OMCL rally, which is a reason to let the DCA gate do its job, not to act ahead of it. Opening a single-name position during an active geopolitical shock. Breaching the cash floor for a discretionary entry. Buying because a clock ran out (rule 16) — applies to policy targets as much as trades. Sizing off any price not live-verified via Robinhood (rule 4). Treating a futures-implied probability (Fed-funds odds) as if it were the settled-close trigger sitting next to it (rule 17) — even as that gap narrows toward the September 16 decision date.

**Governing principle.** *Every deadline expires to no-action. Every repeated flag converts to a rule or retires.
