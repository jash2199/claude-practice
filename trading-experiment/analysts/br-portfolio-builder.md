**Verdict: NO ALLOCATION CHANGE recommended from this desk today.** BW's F grade and the underlying rate-shock thesis are read, credited, and will govern this desk's posture on any future core-up request for as long as rule 6a's pause and the WACC-rebuild clock remain live — but "trim into a closing-bell risk-off print, on one desk's directional read, without this book's own mechanical trigger firing or MS's independent sign-off" is precisely the pressured, same-day sizing this book's rules 4 and 16 were written to prevent. **If BW's F grade persists into a second consecutive report** (its own rule-14 threshold), that is the point this desk commits to revisiting the specific NVDA/VTI/XLE reallocation with a full cross-desk vet, not deferring it a third time.
