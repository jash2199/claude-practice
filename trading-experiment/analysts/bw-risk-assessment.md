## 9. Hedging strategies to reduce the top 3 risks (equities-only toolbox, no options)

**Top 3 risks: (1) NVDA binary earnings/financing-overhang event, (2) blended tech-sector concentration, (3) Hormuz/oil-driven macro shock.**

1. **NVDA event risk**: the only real equities-only hedge is size reduction ahead of the print — there's no options overlay available in this account. A partial trim (not a full exit) ahead of 8/26, sized so the remaining position still participates in upside but the account isn't carrying full exposure through an unpriced binary event, is the cleanest available hedge. This desk flags it again this cycle, with slightly more urgency given today's CDS re-widening; sizing/timing remains the trader's call, not this desk's.
2. **Blended tech concentration**: the standing structural hedge is continuing to prioritize non-tech-sleeve capital deployment (OMCL, XLE, and any future GEHC/PTCT allocation if that cross-vet ever clears) over any further tech-adjacent additions. No action needed today — deployable cash is fully in reserve and no tech add is under consideration — but this stays the standing bias for the next incremental dollar.
3. **Hormuz/oil shock**: XLE remains the only available equities-only hedge instrument. It held its imperfect-but-present decoupling from NVDA again today, a mild positive; but with Trump signaling a possible deal as soon as this week, the trader should also be aware XLE is exposed to *de-escalation* risk (a resolved Hormuz situation would likely pressure XLE further given MS's already-overvalued DCF read), not just escalation upside. No change recommended to the existing XLE position; it's already sized as a partial, not full, hedge (11.80% of equity).

No new hedging trades recommended this cycle — the existing structure (OMCL + XLE as the two non-tech satellites) is the hedge, and it's functioning as intended.
