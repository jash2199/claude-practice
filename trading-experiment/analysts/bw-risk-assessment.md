## Single biggest risk right now

**The AI-slowdown narrative is a new risk category this book has no playbook for, and it hits NVDA and this book's largest sleeve (VTI/VXUS look-through) at the same time, with no catalyst date to underwrite against.** Every risk this desk has priced before — a rate decision, an earnings print, a diplomatic meeting, even the DOJ/Groq probe — has a real or estimable resolution date this book can time a decision around. "The industry's own leaders think development should slow down" does not. MS's read (a sentiment shock, not a fundamentals shock, cash-flow model unchanged) is correct as far as it goes, but it also means the standard tool for absorbing this kind of risk — "wait for the print/decision and reassess" — doesn't apply here. If this narrative converts into actual policy (a lab publicly slowing a roadmap, not just a statement), it becomes a genuine multi-year capex/revenue-growth input this book has never modeled. Layered on top of the still-unresolved Hormuz conflict (no de-escalation, blockade ongoing) and a 10yr sitting at the cusp of 5% for a third straight report, this is one more compounding, unresolved factor, not a replacement for the others.
