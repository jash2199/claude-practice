That is a genuine, dated data point against the "priced for disappointment" framing this desk and JPM both carried into last night, and — per JPM's own read-across — is mild positive sentiment color for NVDA's own 11/17 print (T-13), though not grounds to revise NVDA's thesis on a single night's reaction in two other names.
