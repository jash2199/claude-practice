## Situation update: Hormuz talks harden rather than ease, GEHC data-quality flag fully resolved, CRWD pushes past its own ATH, oil pares a five-session run

**Hormuz/oil — the tape softened its language but Iran's actual position hardened.** Iran's Foreign Ministry stated explicitly today that the US blockade must be lifted *before* Tehran will agree to fully reopen the strait, and FM Araghchi added that reopening also requires eased sanctions and war reparations — a harder, more itemized set of preconditions than the "close to an agreement" framing search results carried as recently as yesterday. Oil still gave back some of its multi-day gain intraday (Oman-Iran shipping-talks progress signs), but WTI closed the prior session at $82.13 (+5%) and Brent sits near $88 — both still near cycle highs. Satellite tracking shows only 8-15 vessels crossing the strait on any given recent day vs. ~130 pre-conflict, with a dozen-plus ships now doing ship-to-ship transfers outside Hormuz entirely — the disruption is deepening operationally even as the rhetoric occasionally sounds conciliatory. Net read: **no change to the rule-3 single-name veto on OXY.** If anything, itemized preconditions (blockade lift + reparations, not just a technical shipping-corridor understanding) push a clean resolution further out than this desk's last two reports implied.

**GEHC — the 8/10 data-quality flag (a $68.02 vs. $72.94 spread with no news to explain it) is now fully resolved, and resolved to the upside.** This run's pull is internally consistent: $73.12 close (8/10), +1.35% on the day, inside the prior 52-week range with room to the $89.77 high. Two fresh analyst actions (Mizuho $85, BTIG $79, both post-earnings reiterations) landed since yesterday, both above the prior $68-73 uncertainty band — the market's own price discovery caught up to the print. Nothing about the CFO-transition resolution (closed 8/10) or the Q2 beat-and-maintain-guide picture has changed; the only change is that the fog has cleared and it cleared in this desk's favor.

**IONQ — price continues to drift, no new catalyst.** $42.79 (8/10 close, down from $44.43 prior close), still inside the post-Wolfpack/Morgan-Stanley-stake-cut trading range. No rebuttal, no fresh allegation, no new institutional move found this run. Risk rating holds at 9.

**CRWD — pushed to a fresh high beyond its own recently-set ATH, insider selling continues on the same schedule.** Intraday reads today show $225.10-225.16 (+4.98% on the session), above the $219.35 all-time high this desk logged as recently as 8/5 — a second leg higher in under a week. This is now more extended, not less, heading into an August 26 print that JPM's brief already flagged as the single most important data point on this name: CRWD's most recent quarter (6/3/26) beat on both lines and still fell 9-11% on "merely in-line" guidance. Nothing here argues for chasing into the print; if anything, a fresh high raises the bar the print needs to clear. Rule 2 (don't chase) stance unchanged.

**MU — CXMT overhang gains a new, more direct thread.** Beyond the second-fab expansion already logged, fresh reporting confirms Apple has been testing CXMT memory chips across iPhone/MacBook lines, and Micron is now actively lobbying the Trump administration to block Apple from using CXMT/YMTC chips in devices sold outside the US — a defensive, not offensive, posture that signals this desk's own concern (a credible Chinese memory competitor at scale) is now shared by Micron's own management, not just outside analysts. Stock $864.35 (8/10), off its June peak near $1,250-1,300. Rule 5 hard pass stands, reaffirmed by MU's own lobbying posture.

**AVGO — six-day win streak continues on a fresh company-specific catalyst, still not a chase-worthy entry.** New VMware vDefend / Avi Load Balancer AI-security upgrades (announced 8/6) are driving the stock, now $420.57, with a 26-analyst consensus PT of $493.24 (88% Buy/Strong Buy). Genuinely good news, but the stock is extended on its own six-day run — rule 2 still says wait for a pullback, not the news flow.

**OXY — Q2 strength and price-target hikes continue to run ahead of what the geopolitical veto allows this book to act on.** Wells Fargo's $79 target (from $72) is the latest in a string this week; OXY itself sits at $58.65. The fundamentals case has been investable-grade for weeks — the entire reason it stays off this list as an actionable pick is rule 3, and today's hardened Iranian preconditions make that veto's logic more current, not less, exactly as flagged in our last report.

**Market/rates backdrop — mixed, not directional.** S&P +0.13%, Dow +0.10%, Nasdaq +0.28%, Russell +0.21% — a choppy, rangebound session (stocks briefly went red intraday as the Hormuz standoff looked more entrenched, then recovered on soft July payrolls data, -23K, reducing near-term hike odds). 10-year Treasury 4.73%, flat vs. every read logged today — still the closest this book has ever traded to rule 6a's 4.75% two-consecutive-close threshold, with tomorrow's (8/12) CPI print the explicit, dated test everyone on this book has now flagged.
