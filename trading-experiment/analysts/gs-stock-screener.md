**Oil itself is not confirming the Hormuz-optimism rally in XLE-adjacent names.** Brent is actually down slightly this morning (~$82.15, -0.41%) even as equities cheer the routing-deal headline — the same headline-vs-oil-price divergence BW flagged as unreconciled on 8/6 (XLE popping on de-escalation language while the underlying commodity doesn't move the same way) is repeating today rather than resolving. This desk treats today's XLE strength, if any, the same way as yesterday's: sentiment on a headline, not a fundamental re-rate, until MS's composite gap actually narrows on real oil-price data rather than routing-diplomacy language.

**GEHC picked up two pieces of incremental, genuinely positive news since 8/6** — the first fresh GEHC developments in over a week. On 8/3 and 8/4 the company expanded its breast-imaging portfolio (Invenia ABUS Prime, ABUS StreamVue — both newly FDA/EU cleared for dense-breast imaging) and its LOGIQ ultrasound family — real commercial pipeline execution, not just guidance restatement. Separately, CFO Jay Saccaro's previously-announced departure (effective 8/14) now has a named interim successor (Controller/CAO George Newcomb), reducing near-term leadership-transition uncertainty. Neither is thesis-changing on its own, but both cut the same direction as the existing bull case (record backlog, reaffirmed guidance) and reinforce that GEHC's six-plus-week rule-6 blockage remains a pure process gap, not a data gap — if anything the data keeps getting better while it sits unactioned.

**AMZN: nothing new since the Bezos filing, and rule 2 still applies.** No fresh news found since the 8/4 $4.07B Form 144 filing and the ~2% post-filing dip; AWS's underlying Q2 strength (37% YoY growth, 39.4% operating margin) is unchanged and still the real story under the insider-sale noise. Not re-ranking it — still unvetted (rule 6) and was already extended before this morning's broader rally, which only adds to that case.

**AVGO/MU relative-value nuance worth logging, not acting on.** A fresh comparative piece (Trefis, 8/6) notes the market is paying ~59.7x operating profit for AVGO vs. only ~17.0x for the faster-growing MU — a real valuation gap between two names already on this list. This does not overturn MS's DCF hard-pass on MU (rule 5 is binary and MS's read stands), but it is a data point worth handing to MS the next time MU's DCF gets refreshed, since a persistently wide peer-multiple gap alongside a "sharply overvalued" DCF read is at minimum worth reconciling rather than ignoring.
