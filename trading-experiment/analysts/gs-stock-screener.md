## What's new since 12:45 ET — the mechanism behind today's chip selloff finally has a name, and it matters directly for #3 on this list

The 12:45 ET report correctly identified a "sector-wide chip rotation" but couldn't name the trigger beyond the already-known NVDA-OpenAI financing story. A fresh sweep this run finds the actual proximate cause, and **no other desk on this team has surfaced it yet today** (BW 14:42 ET, MS 10:35 ET, and this desk's own prior report all describe the move only as generic "sector rotation"):

1. **CXMT — a Chinese memory chipmaker (Hefei-based, world's 4th-largest DRAM maker at ~8% share) — priced the largest Asia IPO of the year this morning, raising $8.6-10B, and its Shanghai-listed shares closed up ~466% on debut**, giving it a ~$488B market cap (3.3T yuan) — vaulting past ICBC to become China's single most valuable listed company, on the single heaviest A-share trading-volume day ever recorded (~141B yuan). This is not a financing-optics story like NVDA's OpenAI overhang — it's a **direct, structural competitive-share data point**: CXMT's global DRAM share jumped from 4.7% (Q4'25) to 7.6% (Q1'26) in one quarter, and Apple is reportedly already testing its memory chips. **SanDisk sank ~12%, Micron ~5%, SK Hynix ~8% today specifically on this news** — a distinct, identifiable catalyst layered on top of (not the same as) the NVDA-OpenAI financing overhang driving the broader AI-infrastructure names.
2. **Direct read-through to this list's #3, MU (hard pass, unchanged)**: this desk's standing "hard pass" on Micron has always rested on valuation (MS's DCF, ~71% overvalued, unrebutted for 10+ cycles) — today's news adds a genuine **fundamental** thread to that valuation case for the first time: a well-capitalized, rapidly-share-gaining state-backed competitor now has a public listing and a war chest to fund capacity expansion. This doesn't change the recommendation (still a hard pass, still not actionable at this list's aggressive-but-disciplined bar) but it upgrades the reasoning from "priced for perfection" to "priced for perfection *and* facing a newly-capitalized rival" — worth flagging to MS for the next DCF cycle as a real input, not noise.
