Today's WebSearch sweep surfaced the stale-PT-cut explanation above; treat the bearish framing as resolved/lagging rather than a live conflict going forward, though this desk will keep checking each cycle in case a genuinely fresh bearish item shows up.

Q2 fundamentals unchanged (record $23.9B backlog, 11% YoY order growth, FY26 guide 3-4% organic growth / $4.80-5.00 adj. EPS / $1.6B FCF reaffirmed). **AVGO — second straight session of real pressure, priority flag reiterated.** $365.80, down from yesterday's ~$380 (a further ~3.7% slide), now ~21% off its early-June peak. Two distinct overhangs compounding: (1) the Marvell-Google custom-AI-silicon competitive threat flagged yesterday, still unresolved and un-walked-back; (2) a broader "reassessing AI growth amid rising bond yields" narrative hitting the whole AI-capex complex today, on top of the standing BofA $370B AI-debt/financing-concentration flag (8/14). Fundamentals (48% revenue growth, 143% AI-chip-sales growth last quarter) remain genuinely strong — this reads as a multiple compression story, not a business deterioration story, but two straight down sessions on stacking overhangs makes this the most urgent unvetted name on the sheet. Sept 2 print, 13 days out.

**MU — stable, no new company-specific news.** $937.11, essentially flat vs. Wednesday's $940.76. Netlist ITC filing (8/12) remains the only live legal overhang, unresolved, no new procedural update found. Rule-5 hard pass unchanged.

**PTCT — quiet cycle, no new information.** $72.63, unchanged. Wells Fargo ($106→$109) and Barclays ($120→$122) PT raises are the only fresh items, both modestly positive, neither changes the tier.

**OXY — steady, one mixed analyst data point.** $59.80 basis unchanged in this desk's last live-ish read; Barclays trimmed its target to $71 (from $75) on 8/17 even as Mizuho's $78 (raised from $75) stands from the same week — a modest sourcing split worth noting but not a directional signal either way. Brent up to ~$93-95 today (from $91.32 Tuesday), a genuine move consistent with continued Hormuz-related supply-risk pricing. Rule-3 hard veto unchanged and, if anything, reinforced by the firmer oil tape.

**XLE — tracking the same crude strength as OXY.** $63.58 (8/19 close per this desk's freshest read), essentially flat vs. Wednesday's $63.68 and consistent with the trader's own live feed. Continues to do its hedge job cleanly.

**IONQ — quiet cycle, incremental positive.** $43.53, roughly flat vs. yesterday's ~$44.12. New this cycle: a signed MOU with CMC Microsystems to put IonQ hardware into Canada's national quantum-computing sandbox — a modest commercial-reach item, not a re-rating catalyst. Morgan Stanley's 5.6% stake disclosure (flagged yesterday) still undigested by the tape. Still rule-6 blocked, now 4+ weeks.

**FRO — the overvaluation gap this desk flagged yesterday just got materially wider, reinforcing the hard-pass rather than reopening it.** Price has run to $44.45 (from $37.68 Tuesday/Wednesday, a ~18% jump in two sessions) on confirmation of the 8/4 news that Frontline will sell two VLCCs for ~$270M and pay a $0.80/share special dividend. Against MS's DCF (~$18.5-27.4/sh under both scenarios tested, unchanged, first-built 8/19), this is now a >60% premium to the bear case and a >60% premium to the bull case at the high end — the widest dislocation on the entire sheet. Per rule 5, this changes nothing about the hard-pass verdict; if anything, a rally driven by a one-time asset sale and special dividend (not a change to the freight-rate cycle MS modeled) is exactly the kind of move rule 5 exists to keep this desk from chasing. Stays at #10, demoted, not reconsidered.

**Macro.** Treasury's bond-buyback intervention (announced 8/19) continues to support the tape — 10yr yield eased further to 4.64% today, down from Tuesday's 20-month-high 4.75% intraday print; rule 6a buffer widening, not narrowing. Futures were mixed at Thursday's open (Dow -0.1%, S&P flat, Nasdaq-100 +0.1%) following Trump's pledge to "squeeze Iran economically." Brent's move to ~$93-95 (see OXY/XLE) is the day's most notable cross-asset confirmation of continued Hormuz-linked risk pricing. NVDA CDS: no fresher print found beyond the 80.77bps level already on file as of 8/18 (still below the 82bps late-July peak/revisit-line) — nothing new to add to BW/MS's existing coverage there. Hormuz/Oman: this desk found nothing more recent than the team's existing 8/17-18 MoU-expired / Trump-Oman-threat coverage already on file from BR/BW/JPM — no incremental development to report this cycle; the most recent concrete diplomatic marker this desk can independently confirm is the 8/5 Iran-Oman route-coordinate agreement, which predates and does not resolve the harder framing the team picked up yesterday.
