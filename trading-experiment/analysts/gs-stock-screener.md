## Situation update: a third straight day of record closes, but the oil tape is quietly arguing against the "clean deal" narrative

Markets extended their rally again Wednesday: **Dow +0.95% to a fresh record 54,597.29 (+511 pts); S&P 500 +0.65%; Nasdaq +0.24%** — a calmer, broader-based grind higher after Tuesday's sharper move, with investors digesting SpaceX and AMD earnings alongside continued Hormuz optimism. **Trump said a deal to reopen the Strait could be announced "Wednesday or Thursday,"** and Axios/Fortune both report the framework under discussion: a 60-day temporary Oman-Iran arrangement (extendable), with inbound traffic routed through an Iran-controlled lane and outbound through an Oman-controlled one. **This desk flags, for a second straight cycle, that the substance looks thinner than the headline**, and today adds a sharper data point: a CNN live update cites a Gulf official putting the odds of a deal actually landing by Friday at only **50-50** despite Trump's public optimism, and Iranian state media is on record saying any Oman-route arrangement has **"no connection"** to the Strait's actual reopening. That is about as direct a contradiction of the "freedom of movement" framing as this desk has seen yet — the market is trading a clean-resolution narrative that Iran's own state media is actively disputing.

**Oil itself is sending a mixed signal worth sitting with.** Brent traded near $79.50 at Tuesday's session low but has bounced to **~$83.72** as of early Wednesday trading — a real move in the "wrong" direction for the reopening thesis, even as broader commentary (easing tensions, a projected 2026 oil surplus) still leans bearish for crude on a multi-month view. This desk's read: the bounce is a mild but genuine tell that Tuesday's oil-price move ran ahead of anything actually confirmed on the ground, consistent with the Gulf-official skepticism above.

**Data-quality correction (self-flagged):** last cycle's report stated a "multi-source-confirmed" AVGO price of $476.15 for 8/4 with high confidence — that figure was wrong. A fresh check today shows AVGO's actual 8/4 range was **$398.62-$422.07, closing at $417.75**. This desk is retracting the $476.15 figure explicitly rather than letting it stand uncorrected in the historical record; treat $417.75 as the reliable 8/4 close, still up meaningfully from the older ~$389 stale figure but nowhere near the erroneous number reported last cycle. This is now the second consecutive-cycle data-integrity issue this desk has had to self-correct (AVGO twice) on top of the chronic OMCL/XLE WebSearch-lag problem BW has separately flagged — worth raising with the desk's sourcing process directly rather than treating each instance as a one-off.

**One incremental, real catalyst worth noting on a name not currently on this desk's top 3:** Jeff Bezos disclosed plans to sell 15 million AMZN shares (~$4.07B), pulling the stock back from Tuesday's $284.82 high to a **$275.82-281.07** range Wednesday — a genuine insider-supply overhang, not noise. This modestly changes AMZN's risk calculus versus last cycle's pure "don't chase a +22.75%-in-a-week move" framing.
