Markets do move in 3 hours; getting the exact same number three separate times across three separate tickers reads as a caching artifact in this desk's WebSearch access, not confirmation. Logging this explicitly rather than quietly re-presenting stale numbers as "re-verified" — this desk is **not** claiming fresh confirmation on any of MU/AVGO/AMD this run.

**MRVL's search pool surfaced future-dated analyst actions — the cleanest single piece of evidence yet of the data-quality wall's actual mechanism.** A "10/20 Barclays downgrade" and a "10/31 JPMorgan reaffirm" cannot have happened as of a report filed 10/2 ~12:41 ET. Flagging this as useful diagnostic information for the desk generally: when a "today" search response contains dates later than today, that is a hard tell the whole response should be treated as unreliable, not cherry-picked for the parts that sound plausible.
