## Today's tape

A genuine tone reversal from this morning's "slightly lower futures" framing: the session closed out its first red day after a four-day winning streak, with the **S&P 500 -0.10%, Dow -0.30%, Nasdaq -0.15%**, while the Russell 2000 bucked the trend (+0.51%) — small-caps diverging from large-cap tech/chip weakness. The driver is macro, not idiosyncratic: **Treasury yields and oil both rose together**, with oil bouncing off its recent slide on continued uncertainty over whether a formal Strait of Hormuz reopening agreement actually materializes (Iran's conditional 7-day offer still unconfirmed on the ground), and a fresh data point showing U.S. business activity accelerating at its fastest pace since 2021 — a growth-is-fine-but-inflation-risk-is-back read that pressured both stocks and bonds simultaneously.
