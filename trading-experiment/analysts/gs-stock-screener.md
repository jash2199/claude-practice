Already held, already at/above BR's target weight — this is a "the thesis keeps surviving contact with a bad tape" note, not a fresh add signal.
2. **SNDK and MU — same NAND/DRAM AI-infrastructure trade, now facing its first real competitive test (CXMT), and the two names split on how exposed they actually are.** China's CXMT reached mass production on a 5th-gen DRAM platform this week (24Gb LPDDR5X chips, ≥50% more dies/wafer) — a genuine headline that hit both stocks and is being cited across financial media as a driver of this week's memory-sector pullback. The critical distinction, confirmed by multiple sources: **CXMT's new output is concentrated in mobile/mainstream DRAM, not the cutting-edge HBM/AI-infra memory driving the bull case for either name**, and CXMT still lacks EUV lithography access — a real structural ceiling on how far it can climb the value chain. SNDK's own book is NAND/AI-datacenter-storage (datacenter segment +1,298% YoY last quarter), a different competitive lane than CXMT's DRAM move entirely. Net: this is a real new risk line to track (log it, don't ignore it, especially for MU's DRAM-heavy 76%-of-revenue mix), but it is not yet a structural break for either name at current valuations (MU forward P/E ~7.5x, SNDK ~7x fwd vs. ~22x memory-sector avg). **MU is JPM's confirmed WAIT into its 9/30 print (6 days out) — this desk agrees on timing: don't chase either name into that specific binary event, but the broader AI-storage screen stays intact.**
3. **AMD/INTC/ARM — the Meta Muse chip rally is real and dated (9/21), but has already run further and faster than this desk is willing to chase at current levels.** AMD crossed $1 trillion market cap this week on a ~5-session ~25% advance; ARM rose 17.2% and INTC 12.1% in the same single-session move, all on the thesis that Meta's new agentic AI app (Muse, currently #1 on the US App Store) validates a fresh wave of CPU/orchestration-silicon demand. This is a legitimate, dated catalyst — not stale recirculation — but AMD's forward P/E of ~56x (trailing ~159x) prices in a lot of that thesis already, and INTC's turnaround (Apple foundry talks, the Musk "Terafab" tie-up, a beat-and-raise quarter) is now up ~300% over the past year with a TTM P/E still negative (recent-loss base) and a forward P/E near 48x. **Actionable stance: watch for a pullback entry, don't chase the gap — these are now momentum names, not value names, and none of them clear this book's own valuation-first standard the way GEHC, SNDK, or MU still do.**

None of AMD/INTC/ARM/AVGO/MRVL/ALAB clear this book's rule-6 cross-vetting gate (MS DCF + BW risk read) this run — they are on this desk's watchlist for the trader's broader awareness, not pre-cleared for a same-run trigger the way GEHC is.
