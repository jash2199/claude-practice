## Headline: the weekend's real story is the Iran/Hormuz de-escalation and the oil crash it triggered — and this desk owes two corrections from the 7/31 report

**Trump halted a planned Iran strike over the weekend, oil crashed, and it's the single most important input across this entire watchlist this morning.** WTI fell ~5.7% to ~$79-80, Brent ~5% to ~$83, both landing right on GS's own long-standing $80 Q4'26 house forecast, on reports of agreed-upon "parameters" for reopening the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for the US easing its naval posture. The mechanical read-through: **every long-oil position in this book (XLE) is now working against, not with, the tape**, while every broad-market and rate-sensitive holding (NVDA, OMCL) gets a tailwind. Treat the de-escalation as fragile and reversible, not as a green light to chase the relief rally.
