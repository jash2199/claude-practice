## Situation update: IONQ's DARPA catalyst is the standout, Hormuz gets messier not cleaner, and a fresh PTCT upgrade earns a look

**IONQ (see #2 above) is this run's headline.** The DARPA contract extension is a durable, non-sentiment data point — government contracts don't get "sold the news" reversed the way a sell-side PT does — but the price has already moved to reflect it. This desk flags the tension explicitly rather than paper over it: strong fundamentals, bad entry-discipline timing. If MS/BW clear the rule-6 gate next cycle, expect them to be sizing against a ~$42 handle, not the ~$36-37 handle every report this week (including this desk's own 09:53 ET one) has been referencing — a meaningfully worse entry math than 24 hours ago, through no fault of the thesis itself.

**Hormuz (see #3 above): this desk is correcting its own morning tone, not just repeating it.** The Iran-Oman shipping-corridor talks are real and continuing, but they now sit alongside a parliamentary push to formally ban US/Israel-linked vessels and a public "theater diplomacy" accusation aimed at the US side — evidence of a messier, more adversarial process than "agreed in principle" implied this morning. Oil itself is mixed on the news: WTI ~$78.07 (+1.01%), Brent ~$82.15 (-0.41%) — a split tape consistent with the deal's genuine but partial, contested nature rather than a clean risk-on or risk-off signal either way.

**PTCT gets a fresh, genuinely new positive data point this run**: Citi upgraded PTC Therapeutics to **Buy with a $108 price target**, citing strong Sephience sales in phenylketonuria (1,647 commercial patients by June 30, >90% renewal rate) and flagging votoplam (Huntington's) and vatiquinone (Friedreich's ataxia) as underappreciated pipeline assets not yet in the Street's numbers. This is the first sell-side upgrade this desk has seen on PTCT since it entered coverage — worth a fresh JPM/MS look given the standing "WAIT" stance predates this upgrade.

**AVGO extends its run rather than reversing**: six straight green sessions, +14% cumulative, market cap now ~$2.0T. Nothing new fundamentally (still the same AI-chip growth story, still the same Conviction List removal flagged last report), but the move itself is now firmly in "already priced in, don't chase" territory per rule 2 — this desk is not raising conviction just because the stock kept going up.

**CRWD**: modestly positive sentiment items (2026 Threat Hunting Report release, Fal.Con 2026 selling out at record pace) drove a +3.5-3.8% pop this week, but nothing that changes the fundamental case — still no MS DCF on file (rule 10 friction), next print confirmed August 26.

Per rule 4, this desk is explicitly not using today's MU price for anything — flagging it as the same kind of stale/inconsistent-search problem MS has repeatedly hit on XOM/CVX, now showing up on a screener name too. **Do not size off any WebSearch MU price this cycle.**

**AMZN, GEHC — reconfirmed, no material change beyond what's already on file.** AMZN: up ~1.4% this run per WebSearch, AWS/Bezos-sale story unchanged. GEHC: today's search reconfirms (rather than adds to) the Patient Care Solutions strategic-review story already flagged last report — Q2 revenue $5.30B, 11% organic order growth, record $23.9B backlog, guidance maintained. Still the most substantive unactioned rule-6 gate on the book (seventh-plus week).
