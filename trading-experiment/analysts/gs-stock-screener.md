## Headline: the AI-safety narrative has grown a second, confirmed leg — a real rotation into cybersecurity, not just a chip-sector selloff

1. **CrowdStrike is not just "less hurt than the rest of the complex" as this desk speculated this morning — it is up ~11-12% today, and it is not alone.** Fresh WebSearch confirms a genuine, broad cybersecurity-sector rally: CrowdStrike (held-candidate, this sheet's #4) +11-12%, Zscaler +12%, Palo Alto Networks +11%, all on the same Amodei/Altman AI-safety-slowdown story that is hammering NVDA/AVGO/MU. The mechanism is coherent, not coincidental: CrowdStrike's own Fal.Con-launched AI-security products (Falcon Guardian, SafeMind, an Agentic Identity Provider explicitly built to police AI agents) are the market's most direct, investable expression of "if AI development is risky, buy the company that secures it." **This is the clearest single-day illustration this desk has seen of the AI-slowdown narrative bifurcating the market into AI-capex losers and AI-safety-infrastructure winners**, rather than being a uniform risk-off across every AI-adjacent name. RBC, Raymond James, and Wedbush all reiterated/raised targets on the name today. This sharply raises the cost of CRWD staying unvetted — this desk is escalating its rule-6 ask again, now with same-day price confirmation of the thesis, not just a pre-print hunch.
2. **The oil shock is intensifying, not stabilizing — Brent is now quoted near $108-110 (a 4-month high), and a merchant vessel was struck inside the Strait of Hormuz today, killing one crew member — the first reported fatality from a direct strike on a commercial vessel in this crisis.** The Saudi East-West pipeline (the main Hormuz bypass, ~5-7M bbl/day depending on source) remains shut with no confirmed reopening date; combined, the two disruptions are reported to remove 30-40% of Gulf crude export capacity. This is a genuine escalation in both magnitude (a new high oil print) and severity (a fatality, not just a supply-route closure) since this morning's 9:42 ET report.
3. **The 10-year Treasury briefly touched/crossed 5% intraday for the first time since 2023 before easing back to ~4.95-4.97%** — multiple sources (CNBC, TradingEconomics) now confirm an intraday print at or fractionally above 5%, materially different from this morning's "cusp of 5%, not yet crossed" framing.
