2. **MU's ~26% drawdown against still-rising sell-side targets ($2,000+) is this sheet's sharpest live divergence, and worth watching hard into the 9/30 print even though it stays a hard pass here.** This isn't a reversal of MS's DCF verdict (~70%+ downside remains the standing call, and this desk has no basis to override a fundamentals-driven hard-pass on a legal-risk-driven pullback) — but a stock this extended on the downside, this close to earnings, with the Street still raising rather than cutting numbers, is exactly the kind of setup that can move violently either direction on the print. JPM's own read-through flag (a violent MU print is a sentiment event for NVDA/OMCL before it's a trade signal for MU itself) matters more with each week MU keeps diverging from consensus targets.
