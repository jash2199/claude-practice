### 1. GEHC (GE HealthCare) — held, BUY / ADD candidate
- **P/E vs. sector**: ~24x forward vs. medtech peers in the mid-20s — roughly in line, but Needham's 9/22 initiation (Buy, $93 PT) and the broader analyst tape (consensus Buy) both sit well above today's $65.65.
- **Revenue growth**: low-single-digit organic (FY26 guide reaffirmed at the 9/9 Wells Fargo conference: 3-4% organic), but backlog +$2.1-2.6B YoY and book-to-bill 1.15x point to accelerating bookings ahead of revenue recognition.
- **Debt-to-equity**: moderate — BW's 10-Q-sourced net debt read (~$8.0B) is the one soft spot; FCF conversion is still ramping toward the $1.6B FY26 guide.
- **Dividend**: the board raised the quarterly dividend 14% this quarter (~0.9% yield) — a small but genuinely improving payout signal, not the reason to own it.
- **Moat**: moderate — imaging/diagnostics incumbency and a large installed base, offset by China weakness, tariff pressure, and an ongoing strategic review of the Patient Care Solutions segment.
- **Bull case (12mo)**: $85-90 (backlog converts, FCF ramp lands, multiple re-rates toward peer medtech; Needham's $93 sits above even this).
- **Bear case (12mo)**: $58-60 (FCF conversion stalls, a genuine guidance cut, or the Patient Care Solutions review produces a negative outcome).
- **Risk 3/10**: still the lowest on this sheet.
- Note for the desk: today's -1.70% move is the position's largest single-session drag of the week and pushes it closer to the book's own $62 revisit line (widened from $65 on 9/15) without breaching it — no trigger live, but worth flagging given how frequently this name has traded near that line this month.
