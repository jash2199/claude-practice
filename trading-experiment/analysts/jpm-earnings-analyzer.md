## Brief 1: NVDA (NVIDIA) — held, T-11, reports Tuesday 2026-11-17 after close

**Report date/time — IR-confirmed, unchanged:** Tuesday, November 17, 2026, conference call 2:00pm PT / 5:00pm ET, covering Q3 fiscal 2027 (quarter ended ~October 25, 2026). **Note on date-conflict sources:** several secondary trackers (OptionsAI, Apexvol, one Tradestie-era aggregator) still list Nov 18 — this remains a stale/incorrect secondary reading, as flagged on prior runs. **1. Last 4 reported quarters vs. estimates:**

| Quarter | EPS (non-GAAP) actual vs. consensus | Revenue actual vs. consensus | Stock reaction (next session/close-to-close) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 FY26 (Nov 2025) | $1.30 vs. ~$1.24 (beat ~4.8%) | $57.01B (beat) | ~-3.2% |
| Q4 FY26 (Feb 2026) | $1.62 vs. ~$1.53-1.54 (beat ~5-6%) | $68.13B vs. ~$66.21B (beat ~2.9%, +73% YoY) | ~-5.5% ("beat but sell off") |

Four-for-four beat on both lines across the last four reports — the pattern that has broken down is the market's *reaction* to the beat, not the beat itself: three of the last four reports were sold despite clean upside, and only the most recent (Aug 2026) produced a clean pop. For context, the quarter before this stretch (Q2 FY26, reported Aug 27 2025) also beat on both lines and still sold off on the print (sources range -2% to -5% depending on measurement window) — so the "beat but sell off" pattern has shown up in 3 of the last 5 prints, not just 2 of 4.

**2. Next-quarter (Q3 FY27) consensus:** Company's own guide (Aug 26 call): revenue $108.0B ± 2% (~$105.8B-$110.2B), non-GAAP gross margin 74.0% ± 50bps, explicitly assuming **zero China data-center compute revenue**. Street consensus as of this run clusters **$104-109B revenue** (one source: $103.95B; another: $108.8B) and **$2.34-2.47 non-GAAP EPS** (narrower spread than prior runs' "$2.30-$2.50 loose band," now anchored by two independently sourced figures: $2.34 and $2.47). Treating the company's own $108.0B ± 2% guide as the more reliable anchor; Street EPS consensus is converging around ~$2.35-2.45.

**3. Key metrics to watch:** (a) Data Center revenue growth rate off the now-larger base (+117% YoY last quarter) — the single biggest swing factor, Data Center is ~92.5% of total revenue. (b) Blackwell/GB300 ramp commentary and any supply-constraint language — a fresh search this run found no November-2026-dated update; background reporting (undated to this week) describes Blackwell variants (B30A) still reaching China through gray-market/smuggling channels despite restrictions, and H200 (not Blackwell) cleared for China under tight licensing/duty conditions — neither is a fresh disqualifier per the contingency plan's own criteria. (c) China/export-policy commentary — guide already assumes zero China data-center compute revenue; no policy change found this run. (d) Gross margin trajectory against 74.0% ± 50bps. (e) Any update to the preliminary FY2028 ~70% YoY growth framing. (f) Capital-return cadence. (g) **New this run, directly tied to the regulatory selloff: any management commentary addressing the post-midterm AI-regulation/export-policy uncertainty that drove 11/5's -9.26% session** — this is now a live, market-moving question the call will almost certainly be asked about, not just a background risk.

**9.
