## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **FY27 Q1 guidance (the real swing factor, not Q4 itself).** Given Q2 FY26's precedent, the forward guide — not the trailing quarter's beat — is what will move the stock. Watch specifically for confirmation of the "low-teens sequential" FY27 Q1 revenue guide GS has modeled, and for any commentary on FY27 capacity being "locked in" (a phrase now circulating in sell-side previews).
2. **HBM4 ramp progress and yield/pricing commentary.** Micron has begun volume production of HBM4 (36GB, 12-high) for Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform — >2.8 TB/s bandwidth, >20% better power efficiency than HBM3E, ramping at ~2x the pace of HBM3E 12-high. Any deceleration or yield/pricing walkback here is the single most sentiment-sensitive data point on the call.
3. **FY27 capex guidance.** Management has already flagged construction-related capex up >$10B YoY in FY27 for HBM/DRAM capacity; watch whether that gets sized more precisely and whether it reads as disciplined expansion or a supply-glut risk being set up 12-18 months out.
4. **Gross margin trajectory beyond the 86% Q4 guide** — whether pricing discipline (multi-year DRAM/HBM agreements, tight supply) holds, or the print simply meets the guided midpoint with no upside surprise (which, per the Q2 precedent, may not be enough to rally a stock that has already run up into the print).
5. **Taiwan labor situation — now a quantified, live risk.** The union's disclosed ask (~83 months' salary one-off bonus for FY26, plus a permanent 15%-of-operating-profit quarterly bonus structure from FY27) is aggressive enough to read as an opening negotiating position rather than an imminent walkout, but a strike vote is now proceeding under formal legal process. Any management commentary addressing this directly on the call — or a strike vote outcome landing before 9/30 — would be a fresh, dated catalyst independent of the print itself.
6. **China/export-control commentary** — standing sector risk, watch for any incremental language.
