## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **FY27 Q1 guidance** — the real swing factor. Watch for confirmation of low-teens sequential revenue growth and FY27 HBM capacity being "locked in."
2. **Management commentary on the two live ITC/Netlist actions.** As of this morning, no new filing activity found beyond what was already known (the August 12 ITC complaint covering DDR5 RDIMM/MRDIMM patents, and the separately-filed HBM-specific action layered on top). A confident, dismissive answer on the call would defuse this quickly; a hedged or evasive one keeps it live as an overhang.
3. **Direct commentary on the Taiwan labor dispute**, now with a modestly de-risking data point: the Taichung union's next mediation session is dated **October 22**, i.e., after tonight's print, so no strike-vote outcome is expected on the call itself. The Taoyuan union's mediation has already broken down and it says it is moving toward a strike vote — watch whether management addresses this directly or continues to defer. Micron's own offer (35-68 months' bonus pay, min. cash comp T$1.7M) remains on the table, unaccepted.
4. **CXMT competitive positioning** — ~10% DRAM share and rising; watch whether management keeps framing the overlap as concentrated in non-premium DRAM rather than HBM/server-DRAM.
5. **HBM4 ramp progress and yield/pricing** for Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform — still the single most sentiment-sensitive data point, now with the added relevance of the HBM-specific patent action.
6. **FY27 capex guidance** — disciplined-expansion framing supports the bull case; anything read as supply-glut risk 12-18 months out would not.
7. **Gross margin trajectory beyond the 86% Q4 guide** — the above-Street target hikes are partly margin-driven.
8. **China/export-control commentary** — standing sector risk, intertwined with the CXMT share-growth story.

- **Trend read-through:** Core Data Center's sequential-doubling pace is the least likely to repeat — tonight's Q4 growth rate there is a real tell for whether the AI-memory supercycle narrative is still accelerating or just compounding off a high base, and HBM sits inside this bucket, with the fresh patent-litigation overhang attached.

## Bull case scenario and price impact estimate

Print beats guidance again (5-for-5), FY27 Q1 guide confirms low-teens sequential growth, HBM4 yield/pricing commentary is clean, margin commentary validates the improved-HBM-margin thesis behind the target hikes, management gives a confident answer on the ITC/Netlist action (framed as standard industry licensing dispute, no near-term supply/customer risk) and acknowledges the Taiwan dispute without appearing rattled by it (helped modestly by the Taichung mediation date landing after, not before, the call). With two desks already at $1,520-1,540 targets, a genuine clean beat-and-raise-plus-reassurance quarter could see the Street average follow rather than lead for once. Precedent (Q1/Q3 FY26 reactions) suggests **+10% to +16%** next-session move is plausible, though the stock would need to reclaim four sessions of pre-print weakness first just to get back to flat.

## Bear case scenario and downside risk estimate

Print merely meets the guided midpoint, and/or FY27 Q1 guidance disappoints relative to the low-teens sequential bar — the Q2 FY26 precedent (beat but -8%, deepening to -20%) is the template, and four straight sessions of pre-print weakness despite bullish price targets suggests the market may already be primed to read an in-line quarter as a disappointment. Downside compounders unchanged from yesterday: (1) any commentary lending credibility to the HBM-specific ITC/Netlist exclusion-order case, or a downstream customer (Nvidia/Broadcom/Google, all named in the filing) making noise about supply-chain risk; (2) an evasive answer on Taiwan, even with the immediate strike-vote pressure nominally pushed to the Taoyuan track rather than a company-wide near-term event. Plausible downside: **-8% to -20%**.
