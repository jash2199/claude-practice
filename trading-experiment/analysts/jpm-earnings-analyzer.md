## Brief 1: OMCL (Omnicell, Inc.) — Q3 2026 CONFIRMED, beat-and-raise

**Report date: today, Thursday, October 29, 2026, before market open. Call 8:30am ET.** This resolves the multi-week date dispute (most sources vs. Earnings Whispers' lone Oct 30 holdout) in favor of the team's long-standing 10/29 convention — the print itself is the final word.

**What changed since 10/28 — the actual numbers, not another estimate roll:**
- **Adjusted EPS $0.58 vs. ~$0.42 consensus** — a decisive beat (~39% per StockStory's tally), well above the company's own $0.35–$0.43 guide band and far above the ~$0.24 "Street" figure this desk carried into the print. **Correction to the record:** that $0.24 figure, carried in the 10/27–10/28 briefs as a Street EPS estimate, appears on reflection to have been a GAAP-vs-non-GAAP mismatch — today's actual **GAAP diluted EPS came in at $0.24** (up from $0.12 a year ago), which is suspiciously identical to the number this desk had been citing as the non-GAAP consensus. Flagging this as a likely sourcing error in this desk's own prior work, not a new finding about the company.
- **Revenue ~$307.85M**, modestly above the ~$304M consensus most sources cite (one outlier, GuruFocus, frames it as a miss against a $310.25M figure — noted, not adopted as the primary read given the majority-source convergence around $304M).
- **FY26 adjusted EPS guidance raised to $2.48–$2.56** (from $2.15–$2.30 at the July print) — a second consecutive guidance raise this fiscal year.
- **FY26 revenue guidance narrowed to $1.225–$1.231B** (from $1.225–$1.245B) — the top end came in, a mild tightening, not a cut.
- **Q4 revenue guide ~$298M, below Street consensus** per StockStory — the same "beat-the-quarter-but-soft-the-next-quarter-guide" pattern this desk has flagged as recurring for OMCL, though this time the full-year number still went up.
- **Market reaction: shares reported +10% premarket** — a positive, not negative, reaction, a reversal from the July print's -13.16% gap-down on a similar "beat but soft next-quarter guide" structure. No live Robinhood-verified price available to this desk (no direct account access); the trader's next run should log the actual verified move.
- **No segment-level breakdown (product vs. services) or bookings figure for the quarter was found in search this run** — flagged as an open item for the next reviewer with Q3 10-Q/earnings-call-transcript access.
- **No confirmation found of memory-chip cost impact landing as guided (~$6M H2 2026 drag)** or of the tariff-refund rolloff tracking as expected — not found, not contradicted.

**1. Last 4 quarters vs. estimates:** Pattern of >10% day-0 moves continues; today's +10% premarket keeps the volatility pattern intact even though the direction flipped positive. Q2 2026 (July): non-GAAP EPS $0.94 vs. ~$0.47-0.48 consensus (beat), revenue $312.2M vs. ~$309.9-310.4M est. (beat), but the quarter included a one-time ~$14.5M tariff refund that inflated the margin beat. Q3 2026 (today): EPS and revenue both beat as detailed above.

**2. Next-quarter (Q4 2026) outlook:** No formal company Q4 EPS guide found in search this run; StockStory's framing is that the ~$298M Q4 revenue guide sits below Street consensus — this is the number to watch into the next print, not today's beat.

**3. Key metrics to watch (updated):** Whether the Q4 revenue softness is deal-timing (as management argued for the bookings-range cut back in July) or demand-related; whether the memory-chip cost drag materializes as guided; full-year bookings range ($425-560M) status — not updated this run.

**4. Segment breakdown:** Not found for the actual Q3 print this run. Guided split from the July release: product revenue $169-172M, services $132-135M — use as a rough prior until actuals surface.

**5. Management guidance from today's call:** FY26 non-GAAP EPS $2.48-$2.56 (raised), FY26 revenue $1.225-1.231B (narrowed), implied Q4 revenue ~$298M (soft vs. Street per StockStory).

**6. Options market implied move:** Standing ~±14.33% (~$5.08) Unusual Whales figure (itself dated to an earlier report cycle, per this desk's repeated caveat) — today's realized premarket move (+10%) sits inside that implied range, on the bullish side of the recent history.

**7. Historical stock price reaction after last 5 reports:** Average absolute day-0 move ~18.4% (carried); July's print moved -13.16%. Today's +10% premarket reaction, if it holds through the full session, would be a milder-than-average, directionally positive outcome relative to that history — a break from the last two quarters' pattern of beating the current quarter but selling off on the next-quarter guide.

**8. Bull case — now largely realized:** The beat-and-raise with a positive premarket reaction is close to the bull scenario this desk described pre-print (raised FY26 guide, positive reaction). Residual upside case: the Q4 softness proves to be deal-timing noise rather than demand erosion, and today's reaction holds or extends through the regular session.

**9. Bear case — not triggered, but not fully retired either:** The soft Q4 revenue guide is the one lingering concern — if it's later read as the start of genuine demand softening (rather than timing), that would be the kind of "bookings/margin deterioration trend" the standing contingency plan treats as a real structural-break signal. Nothing found this run rises to that bar yet.

**10. Recommended play: NO ACTION REQUIRED — the standing OMCL earnings contingency plan's mandatory-re-read trigger (guidance cut, lost contract, or bookings/margin deterioration trend) was NOT tripped by this print; the opposite happened (a guidance raise).** This is a research desk, not a trading desk — no buy/sell/hold instruction is issued here — but the risk flag this desk exists to raise is: **the catalyst has resolved favorably; there is no reason for an emergency review before the next regularly scheduled run.** The one open thread worth carrying forward is the soft Q4 revenue guide, which the team should keep watching rather than treating as resolved.

## Brief 2: SNDK (Sandisk Corporation) — still a preview, 0 days out (after close)

**Report date: today, Thursday, October 29, 2026 — fiscal Q1 FY2027. Call 1:30pm PT / 4:30pm ET, after market close.** Unchanged from the prior cycle's company-IR-confirmed date (September 29, 2026 release). No print has happened yet as of this writing (~09:41 ET) — this remains a pre-earnings brief, not a results update.

**What's changed since 10/28 — one new bullish data point, and a walk-back of last cycle's "tightening consensus" read:**
- **New: management expanded the share repurchase authorization by an additional $14B, bringing total remaining buyback capacity to $15.5B.** This wasn't in this desk's prior SNDK coverage — it's a real signal of management confidence (or at minimum, a tool to cushion the stock against a post-print selloff) and belongs in the bull case below.
- **Correction to last cycle's read: consensus has NOT cleanly tightened toward the top of guide.** This run's fresh search found a wider spread than previously logged: one source cites consensus EPS ~$44.72 / revenue ~$11.16B (revenue above the company's own $10.3-10.8B guide ceiling — likely stale or mis-sourced), another cites EPS ~$41.45 (below the $44-46 guide floor), alongside the previously-logged Hudson Labs (~$46.9 EPS/$10.7B) and Benzinga (~$45.99 EPS/$10.66B) figures that do cluster near the top of guide. **Given this spread, this desk is walking back the 10/27 characterization of consensus as "tightened toward the top of the guide band"** — the picture is genuinely more dispersed than that, not less.
- **Price reference remains unresolved/stale.** This run surfaced a dated $1,403.34 close for October 14 from one source, which does not reconcile cleanly with the previously-carried $1,299.30 (dated 10/16) reference — a later date showing a lower price than an earlier one is internally inconsistent, so neither figure is adopted as authoritative here. The trader's own live-Robinhood price (if SNDK is ever considered for entry) should govern per rule 4; this desk has no account access.
- **No fresher options-implied-move or NAND-pricing-commentary figure found this run** — the standing ~±10.99% (~$182.40) admiralmarkets figure and the Citi/BofA NAND-pricing framing both stand uncontradicted and unconfirmed-fresh.

**1. Last 4 quarters vs. estimates (carried):** Fiscal Q4 2026 (reported August): adj. EPS $39.25 vs. revenue $8.965B — company has raised guidance in 5 of its last 6 updates since May 2025. The August print drew a ~9% premarket drop despite being "mixed" rather than a clean miss.

**2. Fiscal Q1 2027 consensus:** Company guidance (8/5, reaffirmed September): revenue $10.3-10.8B, non-GAAP EPS $44-46. Street estimates are now genuinely dispersed (see above) rather than converged — treat any single cited consensus figure with caution until the print clarifies which was closest.

**3. Key metrics to watch:** Gross margin against the 83-85% guide; December-quarter NAND pricing trajectory commentary; management's response to the CXMT (DRAM) competitive threat; hyperscaler demand commentary; any explicit rationale given for today's $14B buyback expansion (confidence signal vs. simple capital-return mechanics).

**4. Segment breakdown (carried):** Substantially NAND flash and SSD/storage products for AI/datacenter and enterprise customers, ~$42B in contracted multiyear supply agreements as of the last full build.

**5. Management guidance from last call (fiscal Q4, August):** Q1 FY27 revenue $10.3-10.8B, non-GAAP EPS $44-46; reaffirmed September.

**6. Options market implied move:** ~±10.99% (~$182.40) per the admiralmarkets pre-earnings figure — still the best dated-to-this-event figure on file; no fresher number found this run.

**7. Historical stock price reaction after last 5 reports (carried):** Consistently high-volatility; most recent (fiscal Q4, August) produced a ~9% premarket drop on a "mixed" outlook. The stock enters this print down roughly 40%+ off its June all-time high ($2,354.39), on either of the conflicting recent price references noted above.

**8. Bull case (updated):** A clean beat-and-raise with gross margin holding at/above guide's high end, explicit management pushback on the CXMT threat as DRAM-not-NAND, reaffirmed December-quarter pricing commentary — **now reinforced by a tangible capital-return signal (the $14B buyback expansion)** that didn't exist in the prior brief. Given the steep drawdown and low NTM multiple, a clean print plus the buyback backstop could produce a sharper-than-usual relief rally.

**9. Bear case:** Any gross-margin miss relative to guide, management acknowledging CXMT as nearer-term relevant despite sell-side pushback, or signs spot-market NAND softening is spreading into contract pricing. The now-wider consensus dispersion cuts both ways: a weaker print has more room to still "beat" a low-end estimate, but also more room to badly miss a high-end one — this desk reads that as higher binary-outcome risk, not lower.

**10. Recommended play: NO ENTRY — gated by valuation AND the sourced competitive-threat narrative; unchanged.** MS's hard pass on SNDK stands. The buyback-expansion news is a modest incremental positive but does not on its own clear this desk's standing mandate-level pass. After-close print today — the next reviewer should treat tonight's reaction as a fresh data point, not assume today's pre-print framing still holds tomorrow morning.
