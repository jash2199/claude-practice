## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **FY27 Q1 guidance (the real swing factor, not Q4 itself).** Watch for confirmation of low-teens sequential revenue growth and any commentary on FY27 capacity being "locked in" — Baird's thesis (2027 supply-bit growth slowdown) and JPMorgan's (pricing momentum, contract tightness) both hinge on forward guidance holding up, not just the Q4 print clearing its own bar.
2. **Direct management commentary on the Taiwan labor dispute and IPP timing.** With a strike vote and board IPP approval both now confirmed for "early October," expect a direct analyst question on the call. Watch whether management gives a firm date or continues to defer — an unresolved, open-ended answer here is now a real near-term risk, not a background item.
3. **CXMT competitive positioning, updated for the ~10% share figure.** Watch whether management directly addresses CXMT's share growth and reiterates that the overlap remains concentrated in non-premium DRAM segments rather than HBM/server-DRAM — a confident, specific answer would support the bull case; a vague or defensive one would not.
4. **HBM4 ramp progress and yield/pricing commentary.** Micron has begun volume production of HBM4 (36GB, 12-high) for Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform. Still the single most sentiment-sensitive data point on the call.
5. **FY27 capex guidance** — watch whether spending is framed as disciplined expansion (supports Baird's slower-supply-growth thesis) or raises supply-glut concerns 12-18 months out.
6. **Gross margin trajectory beyond the 86% Q4 guide.** Given today's raised targets are partly margin-driven (Baird's "improved HBM margin outlook"), any margin commentary that falls short of that raised bar is a likely swing factor for the stock reaction.
7. **China/export-control commentary** — standing sector risk, intertwined with the CXMT share-growth story.

Print beats guidance again (5-for-5), FY27 Q1 guide confirms low-teens sequential growth, HBM4 yield/pricing commentary is clean, margin commentary validates Baird's improved-HBM-margin thesis, and management gives a confident, dated answer on both CXMT (segment-specificity) and the Taiwan IPP/strike-vote timeline. Precedent (Q1/Q3 FY26 reactions) suggests a **+10% to +16%** next-session move is plausible, though the stock would need to reclaim this morning's pre-print weakness first just to get back to flat.

## Bear case scenario and downside risk estimate

Print merely meets the guided midpoint (no upside surprise), and/or FY27 Q1 guidance disappoints relative to the low-teens sequential bar — the Q2 FY26 precedent (beat but -8%, deepening to -20%) is the template, and today's pre-print weakness despite bullish notes suggests the market may already be primed to read an in-line quarter as a disappointment. Downside is compounded if the call features an evasive answer on the Taiwan dispute, or if the strike vote / board IPP decision lands adversarially in the days immediately around the print rather than being resolved beforehand. Plausible downside: **-8% to -20%**, unchanged from prior reads, with the labor-timeline overlap now a genuine near-term (not just background) risk factor.
