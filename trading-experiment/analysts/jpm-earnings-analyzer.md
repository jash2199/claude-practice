## 3. Key metrics to watch this print

1. **Data Center revenue growth rate** — 93% of total revenue; decelerating meaningfully below the ~100%+ YoY run rate is explicitly named in the book's own contingency plan as a structural-break trigger.
2. **Blackwell Ultra / GB300 ramp and supply commentary** — Q2's call flagged Vera Rubin production shipments beginning and an expectation Vera Rubin would be ~20% of Data Center revenue in Q3; watch whether that materializes or slips. Sell-side (Wedbush, Evercore, Loop Capital) has been citing tightening Blackwell supply as a demand-signal positive into this print — any commentary suggesting supply easing/demand cooling, or a sourced GB300 supply/demand problem, is the plan's explicit sell/trim trigger.
3. **China/export-policy delta** — the August guide assumed **zero** China data-center compute revenue; H200 (not Blackwell) exports to China have been easing under a 25%-fee licensing arrangement through late 2025/2026 per multiple reports, with shipments reportedly resuming to Chinese customers. Watch whether any China data-center revenue gets explicitly included in the new guide (an upside surprise) or whether the assumption worsens (the plan's named trigger) — not whether the zero-assumption itself persists.
4. **Gross margin vs. the 74.0% ±50bps guide** — a miss here alone is a watch item per the standing plan, not an automatic disqualifier, absent one of the structural triggers above.
5. **Q4 FY27 / FY2028 guide** — management's August call already previewed FY2028 revenue growth of ~70%; any walk-back of that framing would be the single most market-moving data point, consistent with the stock's repeated "beat-but-sell-off-on-soft-forward-guide" pattern seen across this book's other holdings (OMCL, GEHC).
