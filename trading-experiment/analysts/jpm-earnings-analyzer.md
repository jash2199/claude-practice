**Pattern: 3 beats, 1 miss on EPS over the last four prints; revenue has been the less reliable line, with aggregator disagreement on 2 of the last 4 quarters** (consistent with this desk's standing rule-4 practice of treating unreconciled aggregator figures as a caveat, not a clean data point). The one miss (Q1 2026) came paired with a guidance cut on input-cost inflation (memory chips, oil, freight) and triggered the worst single-day reaction in the last five prints (-13.2%, below). Q2 2026 reversed that with a clean beat and reaffirmed guidance, but the stock's reaction was comparatively muted (not independently verified this run — flagged as a gap).

### 2. Q3 2026 consensus (for the 10/28 print)

**Aggregator disagreement is the headline here — no clean consensus found.** Benzinga: EPS $1.21, revenue $5.35B. TickFlow: EPS $1.25, revenue $5.41B. A third source (AAII) mislabels a stale Q2 figure ($1.04) as the Q3 estimate and should be discarded. Treating the Benzinga/TickFlow range as the working band: **EPS ~$1.21-1.25, revenue ~$5.35-5.41B** (+~1-2% QoQ off Q2's $5.30B, +~15-16% YoY off Q3 2025's revenue base implied by the $5.07B Q2-2025-equivalent comp cited in the Q2 report). This is a wider-than-usual estimate spread for a single quarter — a flag for how the print could land either side of consensus without being a genuine surprise.

### 3. Key metrics to watch

- **Patient Care Solutions margin/trajectory.** This segment posted a **negative $(26)M segment EBIT** in Q2 (revenue -13.3%, -13.5% organic) and is under strategic review per management's own characterization. A further deterioration here — not a top-line miss elsewhere — is the single most likely source of a genuine structural-break signal (ties directly to this desk's standing OMCL-style contingency-plan criteria: a cut to guidance, a lost contract, or a margin/bookings deterioration trend).
- **Backlog and organic orders.** Backlog hit a record $23.9B in Q2 (orders +11.1% organic). A deceleration here would be the leading indicator the market watches before it shows up in revenue.
- **Inflation absorption.** Management has guided ~$250M of FY26 inflation (memory chips, oil, freight) and flagged no further tariff-refund tailwind after Q2's one-time $107-129M benefit. Watch whether margin guidance (15.4-15.7% adj. EBIT) holds without that cushion.
- **FY26 adjusted EPS guidance band ($4.80-$5.00).** This was cut once already (Q1) and reaffirmed once (Q2). A second cut, or a narrowing to the low end, is the clearest single data point the market will trade on.
- **Sofie Biosciences deal commentary.** The $945M radiopharma acquisition (announced 10/5, closing H1 2027) is pending — any update on financing, dilution, or integration cost on the call is new information not yet priced by either desk's models.

Full-year 2026 guidance **reaffirmed, not raised**, despite the Q2 beat: organic revenue growth 3.0-4.0%, adjusted EBIT margin 15.4-15.7%, adjusted EPS $4.80-$5.00 (midpoint ~$4.90), free cash flow ~$1.6B. Management explicitly cautioned that Q2's tariff-refund benefit ($107-129M) was one-time and should not be modeled forward, and reiterated the ~$250M FY26 inflation headwind. No forward (FY27) commentary surfaced in this run's search — a gap to close on the 10/28 call itself, which should give the first real FY27 framing.

### 8. Bull case

Pharmaceutical Diagnostics' acceleration (+15.6% YoY, 29.6% margin) and record backlog/orders (+11.1% organic) continue, Advanced Imaging Solutions holds its high-single-digit organic pace, Patient Care Solutions shows any sequential stabilization (even flat, not necessarily positive) rather than further deterioration, and management reaffirms or modestly raises FY26 EPS guidance while giving a constructive FY27 framing. **Estimated price impact: +5% to +10%**, roughly in line with the better outcomes in the 5-report history (Q4'25's +4.9%, Q1'25's +3.3%) but with upside skew given the stock is already at a post-entry high and a clean beat-and-raise would be the first in over a year.

### 9. Bear case

Patient Care Solutions posts a second consecutive double-digit organic decline with no stabilization narrative, inflation absorption falls short and management trims the EPS band below $4.80 (a second cut inside three quarters), or the Sofie deal commentary raises financing/dilution concerns. Given MS's DCF already has the fair-value gap at its worst-ever -6.08% and BW has flagged this as the book's top live risk, a disappointing print has less valuation cushion than it would have a quarter ago. **Estimated downside: -8% to -15%**, anchored to the 4/29/26 precedent (-13.2% on a miss-plus-cut) as the realistic worst case and the 7/30/25 precedent (-7.8% on a beat that still sold off) as the base-case downside if the print is merely unspectacular rather than bad.

### 10. Recommended play: **WAIT — hold through the print, no pre-earnings add or trim**

- **Against adding before the print:** BR's standing no-upside-trigger design already governs GEHC (price strength alone doesn't justify sizing up), MS's DCF gap is at its worst-ever reading, and 3 of the last 5 reports were negative reactions including two "beat but sell off" outcomes — adding into that asymmetry ahead of a binary event is not supported by this book's own risk framework.
- **Against trimming before the print:** no structural thesis break exists today (FY26 guidance reaffirmed last quarter, backlog at a record, Pharmaceutical Diagnostics accelerating) — trimming on pre-print nerves alone would violate the same discipline this book applied successfully through OMCL's and NVDA's own earnings contingency plans (rule: a miss or a down-move alone is not a sell signal; only a structural break is).
- **Proposed contingency, mirroring the OMCL/NVDA precedent (for BW/BR to formalize or decline):** treat Patient Care Solutions posting a second straight double-digit organic decline, or a second FY26 EPS guidance cut, as the structural-break trigger for a post-print review — not the headline beat/miss number, and not day-0 price action alone. No same-minute decisions on 10/28 itself; use the next scheduled run after the print, consistent with this book's established pattern (3-for-3 validated on OMCL and NVDA).
