## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **Data Center revenue and the Blackwell Ultra → Rubin transition cadence** — the single largest line and the best read on whether hyperscaler capex is actually translating into booked/shipped revenue, not just announced deals.
2. **China exposure, explicitly.** Management's own $108.0B ±2% guide **assumes zero Data Center compute revenue from China** — any disclosed China revenue this quarter is pure upside optionality the Street hasn't priced, and any commentary on China policy risk matters disproportionately.
3. **Gross margin trajectory** — whether Blackwell ramp costs are still compressing margins or whether the mix has started to normalize.
4. **Q4 FY27 guidance** — the number that actually moves the stock historically more than the print itself (see Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 rows above, both "beat but sold off").
5. **Kress's tone and specificity as interim CEO on her first call** — the team's standing sixth watch item. Listen for whether she commits to a timeline on the permanent-CEO search, and whether she addresses the investigation directly or deflects to the board. A deflection reads differently to the market the day before a Congressional briefing than it would have two weeks ago.
6. **Any forward reference to the 11/18 House committee briefing** or the ongoing Wachtell/Joon Kim investigation — not expected to be substantive, but a flat "no comment" vs. a confident "we expect no material findings" are very different signals for Wednesday.
