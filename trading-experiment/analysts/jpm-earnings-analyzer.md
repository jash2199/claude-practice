### 3. Key metrics to watch

- **Datacenter segment revenue run-rate.** Datacenter went from $213M to $2,977M (14x) over the last four quarters — whether this keeps scaling at anything close to that pace, or decelerates even while still growing, is the single highest-signal line for whether the AI-storage demand story is still accelerating or just normalizing off a tiny base.

- **Consumer segment continued decline.** Consumer revenue fell to $556M (-32%) last quarter as the company's mix shifts hard toward datacenter/enterprise — a further acceleration of this decline (if paired with datacenter deceleration too) would be the first genuinely bearish combination on this name.
