## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **FY27 Q1 guidance (the real swing factor, not Q4 itself).** Given Q2 FY26's precedent, the forward guide — not the trailing quarter's beat — is what will move the stock. Watch specifically for confirmation of the "low-teens sequential" FY27 Q1 revenue guide GS has modeled.
2. **HBM4 ramp progress and yield commentary.** HBM4 shipments for Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform began ramping in March 2026 at roughly 2x the pace of HBM3E 12-high with yields ahead of expectations as of the Q3 call — any deceleration or yield-commentary walkback here is the single most sentiment-sensitive data point on the call.
3. **FY27 capex guidance.** Management has already flagged construction-related capex up >$10B YoY in FY27 for HBM/DRAM capacity; the market will be watching whether that number gets sized more precisely and whether it reads as disciplined expansion or a supply-glut risk being set up 12-18 months out.
4. **Gross margin trajectory beyond the 86% Q4 guide** — whether pricing discipline (multi-year DRAM/HBM agreements, tight supply) holds or whether the printed number simply meets the guided midpoint with no upside surprise (which, per the Q2 precedent, may not be enough to rally the stock).
5. **Any management commentary on the Taiwan labor situation** (see below) — this is now a live, dated risk item the market has not yet had a chance to price against a management response.
6. **China/export-control commentary** — standing sector risk, watch for any incremental language.

## Bear case

A confirmed Taiwan strike (the mediation failure today raises this probability, though a strike is not yet confirmed) would compound this as a fresh operational overhang layered onto valuation fatigue. At the options-implied ~11% move, downside would put MU in the **~$905-920** area; a repeat of the Q2-style ~20% week-long slide would put it closer to **~$810-815**.
