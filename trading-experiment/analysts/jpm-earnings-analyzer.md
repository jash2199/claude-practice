## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **FY27 Q1 guidance (the real swing factor, not Q4 itself).** Given Q2 FY26's precedent, the forward guide is what will move the stock. Watch for confirmation of the "low-teens sequential" FY27 Q1 revenue guide GS has modeled, and any commentary on FY27 capacity being "locked in."
2. **Competitive response to CXMT (new this run).** Management will almost certainly get a direct question about China's CXMT reaching 5th-gen DRAM mass production. Watch for (a) any pricing-discipline language that acknowledges rising Chinese domestic supply, and (b) whether management frames CXMT as a China-market-only dynamic (limited near-term overlap with MU's ex-China customer base) or as a broader capacity/pricing risk. A dismissive non-answer here would itself be a data point.
3. **HBM4 ramp progress and yield/pricing commentary.** Micron has begun volume production of HBM4 (36GB, 12-high) for Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform — >2.8 TB/s bandwidth, ramping at ~2x the pace of HBM3E 12-high. Any deceleration or yield/pricing walkback here remains the single most sentiment-sensitive data point on the call.
4. **FY27 capex guidance.** Management has flagged construction-related capex up >$10B YoY in FY27 for HBM/DRAM capacity; watch whether that reads as disciplined expansion or a supply-glut risk being set up 12-18 months out — a question now sharpened by CXMT's capacity addition.
5. **Gross margin trajectory beyond the 86% Q4 guide** — whether pricing discipline holds, or the print simply meets the guided midpoint with no upside surprise (which, per the Q2 precedent, may not be enough to rally a stock that has already run up hard into the print, and has now pulled back a bit on top of that).
6. **Taiwan labor situation — timeline now confirmed to extend past the print.** The 9/21 Taoyuan mediation ended without agreement; Micron will not disclose its bonus counter-proposal until board approval, now specifically dated **October 9** — 9 days after the earnings print. The union's own strike-vote timeline (early October) could land in the gap between the print and the board decision. Whether management addresses this directly on the call, and whether the strike vote itself lands before earnings, remain live and unresolved.
7. **China/export-control commentary** — standing sector risk, now intertwined with the CXMT competitive question above.
