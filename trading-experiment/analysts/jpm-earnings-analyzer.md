## Key metrics to watch, this specific print

1. **HBM4 ramp for NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform** — ramping ~2x the pace of HBM3E 12-high, yields ahead of expectations as of the Q3 call. Single most AI-capex-sensitive data point on the call.
2. **Data center revenue run-rate** — exceeded $25B in Q3 (>$100B annualized run-rate). Whether it holds, grows, or shows first signs of digestion.
3. **Segment gross margins** — Q3's 83-87% margins across all four BUs were unusually high/uniform; compression would signal pricing giving back some of the AI-driven spike.
4. **DRAM/NAND pricing trajectory** — the beat-and-raise story has been priced-side; any commentary on spot pricing rolling over matters more than a unit-volume miss. **New context this run:** TrendForce's NAND price-growth-forecast cut (~70%→10-15% this quarter) is a live, dated data point management may need to address directly, even though NAND is Micron's smaller segment.
5. **FY27 capex trajectory** — FY26 capex ~$27B net of incentives (~$10B in Q4 alone); FY27 quarterly capex guided above that. A number outrunning revenue growth expectations reads as confidence or overbuild risk depending on accompanying demand commentary.
6. **Tariff/trade commentary** — the Lutnick semiconductor tariff review (potentially extending to servers/laptops) still has not been addressed by MU management on any call; no fresher developments found this run (last dated coverage ~9/2-9/3).
7. **NEW this run — Taiwan labor mediation outcome (2026-09-21, 3 days out, predates the print by 9 days).** Whether the second mediation round produces a deal or triggers a strike-authorization vote is a genuine near-term operational risk with its own catalyst date, ahead of and independent of 9/30. Management will very likely face questions on it on the earnings call regardless of how the 9/21 mediation resolves.

## Bull case

A clean beat-and-raise (Q4 actual above the $50B/$31 guide, or above GS's $51.9B modeled figure) plus a strong initial FY27 Q1 guide (low-teens sequential, per GS) and reassuring HBM4/Vera Rubin yield commentary, with a resolved (not escalated) Taiwan labor situation and no incremental tariff damage, could plausibly repeat the Q3 FY26-style reaction: **+13-16%**. Off the **~$977 base** (yesterday's confirmed close, carried forward given today's price-data dispersion — see Decision summary item 1), that puts the stock in the **~$1,105-1,133** area same-day, still meaningfully below the ~$1,513 average sell-side target.

## Bear case

Given no recent miss to calibrate from, the more probable bear path remains Q2 FY26's pattern: a beat that fails to clear an already-richly-priced bar (now with an explicit ~11% implied move to beat, the highest logged this cycle) — compounded by three concrete overhangs converging in the same window: (1) the still-unaddressed Lutnick tariff review, (2) the AI-capex-digestion narrative that has already produced multiple single-session double-digit-percent swings in MU this month, and (3) **the new Taiwan labor mediation deadline (9/21) sitting just 9 days before the print itself** — a failed mediation and strike-authorization vote would be a genuine, dated operational risk the market has to price before management even takes the stage on 9/30. A guide that merely meets rather than beats FY27 Q1 expectations, adverse Taiwan-labor news, or any margin-compression/tariff commentary, could plausibly produce a **-8% to -18%** move (widened 2pp at the low end vs. yesterday given the new labor-dispute overlay). Off the ~$977 base, that's roughly **~$800-899**.
