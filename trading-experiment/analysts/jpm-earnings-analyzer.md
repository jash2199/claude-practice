## Brief 3: SNDK (Sandisk Corporation) — update (screener name, unheld)

**Report date: Thursday, October 29, 2026, 1:30pm PT call (after market close), fiscal Q1 2027. 13 days out.** Re-verified; no change.

**What's changed since the 10/15 brief: NAND pricing data refreshed, directionally supportive of the bull case, no change to the gating valuation call.** This run's dedicated NAND/DRAM pricing search found TrendForce's most recent Q4 2026 (calendar) forecast — reported 9/30/26 via a Chinese-language tech outlet (ifeng), single-sourced, not independently corroborated this run — projecting NAND contract prices rising another **+15-20% QoQ**, continuing (at a decelerating rate) from Q3 2026's reported +10-15% and Q1 2026's +85-90% pace. - Datacenter segment revenue run-rate (14x YoY last quarter) and gross margin trajectory (84.6%, up from ~26%) remain the two highest-signal lines to watch on the call.
- Bull case: +10% to +20% (datacenter re-acceleration, no pricing-crack commentary — now modestly reinforced by this run's NAND-pricing-still-rising data point). Bear case: -5% to -15% (any commentary suggesting NAND pricing normalization or datacenter deceleration).
