## Key metrics to watch, this specific print

1. **HBM4 ramp for NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform** — shipments began March 2026, company says ramping at ~2x the pace of HBM3E 12-high with yields ahead of expectations as of the Q3 call. Any yield or ramp-pace commentary (positive or negative) is the single most AI-capex-sensitive data point on the call.
2. **Data center revenue run-rate** — exceeded $25B in Q3 (annualized run-rate >$100B). Whether this holds, grows, or shows the first signs of digestion is the number markets will react to fastest.
3. **Segment gross margins** — Q3's 83-87% margins across all four business units (CMBU, CDBU, MCBU, AEBU) were unusually uniform and unusually high; any compression signals pricing giving back some of the AI-driven spike.
4. **DRAM/NAND pricing trajectory** — the entire beat-and-raise story has been priced-side (mix and pricing, not just volume). Any commentary suggesting spot pricing is rolling over matters more than a unit-volume miss would.
5. **FY27 capex trajectory** — FY26 capex guided to ~$27B net of incentives (implying ~$10B in Q4 alone); management has already flagged FY27 quarterly capex above that level, with construction capex driving over half the increase. A capex number that outruns revenue growth expectations would read as either confidence or overbuild risk depending on demand commentary alongside it.
6. **Tariff/trade commentary** — the newly-named Commerce Secretary Lutnick semiconductor tariff review (flagged by GS 9/15, extending potentially to downstream products like servers and laptops) has not yet been addressed by MU management on any call. This print is the first opportunity for the company to comment directly.

## Bull case

A clean beat-and-raise (Q4 actual above the $50B/$31 guide, plus a strong initial FY27 Q1 guide) that also delivers reassuring HBM4/Vera Rubin yield commentary and no incremental tariff damage could plausibly repeat the Q3 FY26-style reaction: **+13-16%**, which off a ~$934 pre-print base would put the stock in the **~$1,055-1,085** area same-day. Sell-side's existing average PT of $1,513 (Strong Buy consensus, per GS 9/15) implies the Street already sees room well beyond a single good print — a bull scenario here is "the next leg of that thesis gets confirmed," not "the thesis gets created."

## Bear case

Given no recent miss to calibrate from, the more probable bear path is Q2 FY26's pattern repeating: a beat that fails to clear an already-fully-priced bar, compounded this time by two live overhangs neither of the prior four prints had to contend with — the named Lutnick tariff review and the broader AI-capex-digestion narrative that already produced a -5.2% single-session move on sentiment alone (9/14, pre-earnings, no company-specific news). A guide that merely meets rather than beats FY27 Q1 expectations, alongside any margin compression or tariff-cost commentary, could plausibly produce a **-8% to -16%** move (sized off the same historical volatility band as the bull case, applied to the downside, since no recent miss exists to anchor a worse outcome). This desk has no basis to call a deeper structural break more likely than that without an actual negative catalyst surfacing between now and 9/30.
