## Key metrics to watch this print

1. **Data Center revenue growth rate** — running at +106-117% YoY the last two quarters and ~92.5% of total revenue; a deceleration meaningfully below the ~100%+ run rate remains one of the plan's named structural triggers and the single biggest swing factor.
2. **Gross margin vs. the 74.0% ± 50bps guide** — a miss alone is a watch item per the standing plan, not an automatic disqualifier absent one of the other triggers.
3. **China/export-policy commentary** — the guide already assumes zero China Data Center compute revenue; the signal is a *worsening* of that assumption.
4. **Blackwell/GB300 ramp commentary** — any credible, sourced supply or demand problem is a named trigger.
5. **FY2028 growth-rate guidance** — last call guided ~70%; reaffirming, raising, or walking it back matters more than the Q3 beat/miss headline itself.
6. **NEW, and now the single highest-priority item on this list: how Colette Kress runs her first earnings call as CEO.** This is not one of the plan's five pre-written structural triggers, but it is the most consequential new variable this print carries. Kress has been CFO since 2013 — a known, credible operator, not an unknown placeholder — and the specific things to listen for are: (a) does she address the leadership transition and permanent-CEO search directly and with a clear timeline, or does she deflect; (b) does guidance language carry any hedging or caveats that read as transition-driven uncertainty rather than ordinary conservatism; (c) does she take ownership of forward guidance in the same voice NVIDIA has used under Huang, or does the market read a tone shift as a signal of internal uncertainty; (d) any analyst questions specifically about board search progress, interim-vs-permanent framing, or whether Huang retains any informal role. BR's own framing (11/12) names this exact axis — "operational continuity... a clean Data Center number, no guidance walk-back, no sign of a leadership vacuum" — as its explicit post-print decision trigger, independent of the five structural items above.
7. **The congressional closed-door briefing, now dated 11/18 (one day after this print).** Three House committee chairs asked Huang to accept that date (or propose an alternative) by 11/11; this run's search still found no confirmation of his response, and the question is now complicated by the fact that Huang is no longer CEO as of 11/11-12 — it is genuinely unclear whether the briefing proceeds with Huang in a personal capacity, is rescheduled, or is reframed around Kress/the company instead. This remains the single most unresolved near-term governance item and sits awkwardly adjacent to the print.
8. **Status of the board's Wachtell Lipton-led investigation** (opened 11/8, outside counsel retained 11/10) into the alleged foreign-intelligence-linked matter. The resignation itself is now a confirmed fact (per the 8-K); whether the underlying espionage allegation is ever substantiated or cleared remains open and is the plan's governance disqualifiers (g)/(h) — a confirmed national-security breach or a substantiated espionage finding, neither of which has fired as of this brief. China's Foreign Ministry has publicly called the espionage claims unsubstantiated; Nvidia's own board statement does not address the intelligence allegation in detail.

## Bull case

Kress delivers a clean, confident first call: clear continuity messaging, a credible permanent-CEO search timeline, no guidance hedging read as transition-driven. Data Center sustains or reaccelerates above the ~100%+ YoY run rate, the company beats the $108.0B±2% guide toward the ~$108.8-109.0B consensus read, gross margin holds at/above 74%, FY2028 growth guidance is reaffirmed, and no adverse incremental development surfaces on the congressional-briefing/board-investigation front. **Estimated price impact: a repeat of the August pattern, roughly +5% to +8% on the print itself**, with the added psychological tailwind of the market concluding the leadership transition is a non-event operationally. This is the scenario that would most directly validate BR's 11/12 target-cut-not-sell decision and MS's "moat/backlog intact, Kress is a credible operator" framing.

## Bear case

Kress's call reads as uncertain or overly cautious — hedged guidance language, deflected questions on CEO search timeline, or any hint of internal disruption — independent of the hard numbers. Layer on top any one of the five named structural triggers (Data Center deceleration, a guide miss, a negative China/export development, a sourced Blackwell/GB300 problem, a material gross-margin miss) or a fresh governance headline (an adverse investigation finding, an unresolved/contentious congressional-briefing outcome, any credible reporting of boardroom discord over the CEO search). **Estimated downside risk: a repeat of the February 2026 pattern (-5% to -9%+ two-day move) is plausible even on a technical beat**, and this time it would be compounding on top of a stock already ~30% below its October 6 record close (per 11/10-dated reporting) rather than starting from a fresh high. A governance disqualifier has *already fired* once (resignation); the two that remain live — (g) a confirmed national-security breach, (h) a substantiated espionage finding — would be a second, independent reason to force a trim/exit review regardless of how the print itself lands, since they are named separately from the print-driven triggers.

## Risk note (JPM house stance)

This remains the single largest position in a small account heading into a binary event, now 4 days away, with the options market still pricing roughly historically-average size (±7.9%). The empirical record argues for real caution on its own terms: four of the last five beats were followed by the stock falling or giving back its gains, including one -9%+ two-day drawdown on a print widely described as a historic beat. **This cycle stacks a second, independent layer of risk on top of that pattern: for the first time, a named structural disqualifier in the trader's own contingency plan has actually fired** (the resignation, now primary-source confirmed via NVIDIA's own 8-K, not just multi-outlet convergence), and the print itself will be run by an untested-in-this-specific-role interim CEO, five days after that fact became public, with a congressional governance matter unresolved one day later. BR's decision to cut the pool target rather than force an immediate sell is the correct mechanism for a confirmed fact without being a reactive panic trade — but it is a conscious risk choice, not a resolved situation. Earnings are exactly where undiversified, concentrated small accounts get hurt; this print now carries both the stock's own well-documented "beat but sell off" history and an active, confirmed governance event layered on top of it.
