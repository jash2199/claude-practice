### WACC rebuild

- Risk-free rate: **5.19%** (10/14-dated, most recent reliably-dated figure on file — today's dedicated Treasury search again found nothing dated this week; not adopted per rule 4)
- Equity risk premium: 4.5%
- Beta: 0.80 (defensive med-tech)
- Cost of equity: 5.19% + 0.80 × 4.5% = **8.79%**
- Cost of debt (pre-tax): 5.0%; after-tax at 20.5%: **3.98%**
- Capital structure: net debt $8.0B / market cap ~$36.2B → debt weight **18.1%**, equity weight **81.9%** (lower leverage than the prior build, mechanically, because the price has risen — not a re-rating of risk per se)
- **WACC (base): 0.819 × 8.79% + 0.181 × 3.98% = 7.9%, rounded to 8.0%**

### DCF vs. market price

At $75.725, GEHC trades **above this desk's base-case fair value ($71.2) but well inside the bull case ($93.5)** — a genuine close call, not a clean mispricing in either direction. This is a material change from the prior report's -10.53% overvaluation call: the beat-and-raise did real work on the numerator (higher FCF trajectory, margin recovery confirmed for at least one quarter) even as the price also moved up 6.49% today.

### Verdict: ~FAIRLY VALUED, tilted slightly overvalued at base case

Unlike ALAB (hard pass, robust across a wide error band) or OMCL (clean undervaluation), GEHC now sits in the same category XLE and MU occupied earlier in this book's history before clearing further review — a defensible call resting on a few judgment-heavy assumptions (margin durability, the net-debt figure's staleness, the terminal growth/WACC pairing) rather than a wide, robust gap. **This desk is not calling it a clean buy or a clean sell; it is calling the prior "-10.53% overvalued" verdict closed out by today's print.**

### Key assumptions that could break this model
- **Net debt is stale (Q2 10-Q, ~$8.0B)** — the Q3 10-Q, once filed, could move this materially; a $1B swing in net debt moves fair value by roughly $2/sh at this share count. This is the single most important open item for the next GEHC-focused run.
- **The $55M tariff refund is non-recurring** — if next quarter's FCF reverts to a run rate meaningfully below today's $793M print absent the refund, the FCF trajectory above would need to be trimmed.
- **Margin durability** — one quarter of 180bps EBIT margin expansion is encouraging but not yet a trend; the bear case assumes it partially reverses.
- **Shares outstanding is back-solved, not 10-Q-verified this run.**
