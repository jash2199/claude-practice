### WACC estimate

Base case WACC **10.5%** — investment-grade balance sheet, strong FCF conversion, and a dividend payer argue for a lower discount rate than NVDA's 11.59%, but customer concentration (three hyperscaler/AI-lab counterparties driving the bulk of forward growth) and sector cyclicality argue against going materially lower. Terminal growth rate **g = 4.0%** — above a textbook 2.5-3% to reflect a still-expanding custom-silicon TAM, not an indefinitely-compounding AI boom.

### Verdict: UNDERVALUED at base case, but flagged as a lower-confidence first build, NOT yet a rule-6 clear for sizing

This is a genuinely defensible base case — the $115B/FY27 and ~$230B/FY28 AI revenue figures are the company's own guidance, not this desk's invention, and the FCF-margin assumption merely extends an already-demonstrated 46% quarterly conversion rate. It is **not** a hard pass like MU or FRO. But unlike this sheet's six holdings (all rebuilt from filings this desk has re-verified at some point), this AVGO build leans on three estimated balance-sheet/share-count inputs and a price reference this desk cannot currently reconcile ($301.58 vs. $336.17, an 11.5% spread between two "fresh" numbers). **Recommend: one more build cycle once FY2026 closes and initial FY2027 guidance is given alongside the 12/9 print, plus a live Robinhood re-quote to resolve the price discrepancy, before this clears rule 6 for an actual sizing decision.**

### Key assumptions that could break this model
- **Share count and net debt are desk estimates**, not pulled from a freshly re-verified 10-Q this run — the single biggest confidence gap on this build.
- **The FY2027E/FY2028E total-revenue figures blend a company-guided AI number with this desk's own non-AI/software growth assumption** — if non-AI segments decline faster than assumed (plausible, given legacy semiconductor softness elsewhere in the sector), total revenue could land meaningfully below this build.
- **Customer concentration**: the AI growth story depends on continued capex commitment from a small number of hyperscaler/AI-lab customers (Google, Meta, OpenAI) — any one of them slowing custom-silicon spend would disproportionately hit this name versus a diversified semiconductor peer.
- **The two live price references disagree by ~11.5%** ($301.58 vs. $336.17) — until resolved, treat any single-point gap percentage above with real caution.
