### 5-year projection (full rebuild)

Base case assumes FY26E revenue at the new guide's midpoint (**~$1.228B**), with growth tapering from the beat-and-raise's current momentum toward a mid-single-digit long-run rate as the pharmacy-automation capex cycle matures, and non-GAAP EPS growing faster than revenue on continued operating leverage — consistent with today's print, where EPS guidance rose ~13% at the midpoint on essentially flat revenue guidance. FCF is not separately guided by the company; this desk assumes FCF conversion starting at 85% of non-GAAP net income (reflecting ongoing hardware-deployment capex and working-capital build behind the stronger bookings outlook) and rising toward 90% as margins mature — an estimate, not a sourced figure, flagged as a key assumption below.

### WACC rebuild

- Risk-free rate: **5.19%** (10/14-dated, standing — no fresher Treasury print found this run either)
- Equity risk premium: 4.5%
- Beta: **1.05** (small/mid-cap healthcare IT, somewhat more volatile than this book's megacap names)
- Cost of equity: 5.19% + 1.05 × 4.5% = **9.92%**
- Cost of debt (pre-tax): 5.5% (small-cap credit); after-tax at 21%: **4.35%**
- Capital structure (gross, market-value basis): debt $168M, market cap ~$1.74B → debt weight **8.8%**, equity weight **91.2%**
- **WACC (base): 0.912 × 9.92% + 0.088 × 4.35% = 9.43%**

### DCF vs. market price

At $38.215, OMCL trades **below this desk's base-case fair value ($46.75) by a wide margin, and below even the bear case is a close call, not a given**. The bear case ($35.24) sits just below today's price — meaning if the soft Q4 guide turns out to be real demand erosion rather than deal-timing noise (JPM's own open question), this name's margin of safety largely evaporates. That's a materially different risk profile than the pre-print +32.49% read, where even the bear case likely stayed well below price.

### Verdict: UNDERVALUED, still the book's widest mispricing — but with a real bear-case tail now

This is not the same clean, wide-margin undervaluation case OMCL was carrying into the print. The beat-and-raise did real, confirmed work (raised EPS guide, narrowed-not-cut revenue guide), but the +6.30% price pop plus this desk's own more conservative, from-scratch FCF-conversion assumption set (since the original $49.1 build's assumptions aren't recoverable from this desk's files) together close a large chunk of the prior gap. **Base case says still meaningfully cheap (+22.3%); the bear case says the margin of safety is thinner than the headline number suggests.**

### Key assumptions that could break this model
- **FCF conversion (85%→90% of non-GAAP net income) is this desk's own estimate, not a company-guided figure** — OMCL does not separately guide full-year FCF; a materially different conversion rate moves fair value meaningfully given the small share count.
- **Balance sheet is Q2-dated (6/30/26); Q3's 10-Q has not been independently reviewed by this desk** — a material swing in net cash (working-capital build behind the stronger bookings outlook could consume some of the $124.2M cushion) would move fair value directly.
- **The Q4 revenue guide softness (~$298M, below Street) is the single most important open thread** — if it resolves as genuine demand deceleration at the next print rather than deal-timing noise, the bear case ($35.24, already close to today's price) becomes the operative scenario, not the base case.
- **Shares outstanding (45.5M) is the most recent dated figure (8/28/26), not Q3-10-Q-verified this run.**
