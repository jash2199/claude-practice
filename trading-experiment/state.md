## Strategy & theories (curated playbook — rewritten in the 2026-07-31 weekly distillation; full run-by-run reasoning stays in git history)

**Core, proven rules (keep applying):**
1. **No fresh catalyst, no trade.** Don't add to or trim a position without a genuine new development since the last run. A quietly-sitting gain/loss is not itself a reason to act. This is the single biggest reason "many runs correctly result in no trade" — held for essentially every run of week 3 outside the two pre-committed trigger fires.
2. **Don't chase an extended move.** A catalyst already 3%+ priced in by the time this run sees it is confirmation, not edge. Prefer pullback/consolidation entries over buying mid-spike. Reconfirmed this week on AMZN (a ~15% single-session move, correctly logged as watch-not-chase by GS itself) and IONQ (SkyWater-close pop).
3. **Geopolitical/macro-shock veto (single-name only).** Don't open a new single-name idiosyncratic position (OMCL, OXY, MU, etc.) during an active, still-unfolding shock. Diversified basket buys (VTI, VXUS, and by extension any broad ETF) are exempt — their price already reflects the market's live view of the crisis. Validated repeatedly (VTI 7/15, VXUS 7/16, VTI 7/21, VXUS 7/30) with no downside surprises traceable to this reasoning.
4. **Never size off a report or web-snippet price — verify live via Robinhood first.** OMCL's WebSearch price has now been wrong for 12+ consecutive cycles running; XLE's WebSearch price is also chronically stale. Treat any WebSearch-sourced price on a live holding as presumptively stale until proven otherwise, and never size off it.
5. **A DCF "sharply overvalued" call is a hard pass, full stop** — regardless of how good a screener's momentum/technical case looks (MU, ~70%+ DCF downside, never traded despite weeks as a top-3 GS idea, including a real demand-driven spike that itself round-tripped this week). Conversely, a DCF that *agrees* with a screener pick (OMCL, then XLE) is a strong green light, not proof by itself — still needs its own entry discipline.
6. **New analyst ideas need one full cross-vetting cycle before sizing** (MS DCF + BW risk read), even under time pressure. Applied consistently to OXY, CRWD, PTCT, EXEL, IONQ, and — this week, successfully — XLE, which cleared the gate 7/24 and fired its entry trigger 7/28. AMZN is now in the pipeline, unvetted.
